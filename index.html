📌 Formatting Rules

  • # Title = Heading
  • ## Title = Subheading
  • 1. text = Numbered list
  • - text or * text = Bullet point
  • A short ALL CAPS line = Heading (automatic)
  • A short line ending with : = Subheading (automatic)
  • Empty line = Paragraph gap
  • With the Multicolour pen: Definition: ... = green, Warning: ... = red

Help & About

WriteMyNotes AI turns your text into handwriting-style digital pages. This is not real human handwriting, just digital text styled to look like handwriting.

Everything runs in your browser. Your text never goes to any server.
